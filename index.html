06 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is YDVCH?

YDVCH is a blockchain project being developed around Proof-of-Work consensus, transparent monetary rules and future real-world utility.

What is the maximum supply?

The planned maximum supply is 1,000,000,000 YDVCH.

Is YDVCH a token?

The project is being designed as its own blockchain with a native coin rather than a token deployed on another chain.

When will the mainnet launch?

Mainnet timing will be announced after private/public testnet validation and security review. No fixed date is being promised yet.
